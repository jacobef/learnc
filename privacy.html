Privacy & level replays

When you press Check, this tutorial sends a replay of your activity in that level to help improve the lessons. It includes your code, variable edits, hints, button clicks, and Check results, with time measured from the start of the level.

We don't store your source IP or attach a visitor identifier. Replays from different levels aren't linked. We don't include cookies, other levels' progress, browser details, page query strings, or real-world timestamps in replay files.

Your code and other lesson text are included, so please don't enter personal information there. Hosting providers receive network addresses to deliver requests; our collector excludes its request logs. Replay files are private; no automatic deletion schedule is currently configured.

Replays are sent in the background. Failed uploads don't affect your answers or progress. The sandbox isn't recorded.

This preference is stored only in this browser. Turning it off also stops recording in other open lesson tabs.
